## Key Question 1: What do AOneSchool (AOne) and "LittleTree" offer, who uses them, how are they priced?

### Takeaway
AOne (formerly AOneSchools, My AOne Learning Sdn Bhd, Malaysia, founded 2018, 500 Global-backed) is the most visible Malaysian learning-centre platform, claiming 4,000+ education providers across SEA, with LHDN e-invoicing, Billplz/iPay88(Payex) in-app payment, multi-branch and a parent/teacher app on iOS/Android/Huawei; pricing is quote-based by student count and not published. No vendor named "LittleTree" could be found in Malaysia or Singapore; the name almost certainly refers to LittleLives (Singapore, preschool-focused, 1,500+ schools, ~293 preschools in Malaysia, KL office), whose pricing is also quote-based.

### Inferences
- AOne is the incumbent a Malaysian tuition-chain owner is most likely to be pitched: broadest segment coverage, multi-branch, LHDN e-invoice already live (as a Premium add-on), and a mature parent app. Its weaknesses from a buyer's view are opaque pricing (quote by student count), e-invoice gated behind Premium, and reliance on third-party gateways (Billplz / iPay88-Payex) rather than a native FPX product.
- The "4,000+" claim has grown from "2,000+" in older copy without an audited basis; treat as vendor marketing.
- LittleLives is a preschool tool, not a tuition-centre tool; a tuition chain would only encounter it if it also runs kindergartens.
