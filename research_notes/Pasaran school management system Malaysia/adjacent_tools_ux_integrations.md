# Adjacent (non-education) software to study for a Malaysian multi-branch tuition-centre management system — features, UX patterns, pricing, integrations (as of Sept 2026)

**Method / caveat for the report writer:** Research was done 29 Sept 2026. Almost every vendor primary page (developers.facebook.com, respond.io, sleekflow.io, curlec.com, chip-in.asia, hasil.gov.my, cleartax, malaysiapg.com.my, twilio.com) was **blocked by the network egress proxy**, so most figures below come from search-engine summaries of vendor pages and from third-party/aggregator blogs (often competitors' comparison pages). Every number should be treated as **"reported, unverified against the primary page"** unless marked otherwise. Prices are volatile (Meta changed WhatsApp pricing 1 Jul 2025 and again 1 Oct 2026; LHDN changed the e-Invoice threshold on 30 Aug 2026). Re-verify before pricing a product.

## KQ1. Conversational CRM / WhatsApp inbox (Respond.io, SleekFlow, Wati, Interakt, Twilio/Meta Cloud API, Chatfuel, Kommo) + Meta WhatsApp pricing in Malaysia

### Takeaway
The "shared WhatsApp inbox + contact merge + lifecycle pipeline + broadcast + workflow automation + AI reply assist" bundle is now a commodity feature set priced at roughly RM130–RM470/month base (Wati RM299, SleekFlow RM469, Respond.io USD79 on annual) plus Meta's per-message fees, which in Malaysia are reported at ~RM0.35 per marketing template and ~RM0.056 per utility/authentication template, with free-form service replies becoming chargeable (after 1,000 free/number/month) from 1 Oct 2026. For a centre-management product the play is not to rebuild a full CRM but to (a) own the parent contact record and lifecycle stage, (b) send utility-class templates (fee reminders, attendance, class changes) via the Cloud API, and (c) integrate with Respond.io/SleekFlow/Wati via webhooks for the human inbox.

### Inferences (what to steal)
- **Design the parent record as a "contact with lifecycle stage"**: Enquiry → Trial booked → Trial attended → Enrolled → Active → At-risk → Withdrawn. Respond.io's Lifecycle and Kommo's Digital Pipeline both make *stage change* the trigger for automations; a centre product should expose the same trigger list (stage entered, N days in stage, no reply in X h) to workflows.
- **Contact merge is non-negotiable**: parents message from two numbers, a grandparent pays, and CTWA ads create duplicates. Copy Respond.io's rule that merge closes the secondary open conversation and keeps one timeline.
- **Category-aware template strategy** driven by the 6x price gap: fee reminders, attendance confirmations, timetable changes, make-up-slot offers should be authored as *utility* templates (≈RM0.056), while promos/holiday-programme blasts are *marketing* (≈RM0.35). Build a "template category" field into every automated message and show per-branch monthly WhatsApp spend.
- **Budget for the Oct 2026 service-message change**: a 300-student branch with chatty parents can exceed 1,000 free replies/number/month; multi-branch centres with one number per branch effectively get 1,000 free per branch, which argues for **one WABA number per branch**.
- **Exploit the 72-h CTWA window**: run trial-class ads as Click-to-WhatsApp, and have the system auto-reply within 24 h so the whole enquiry-to-trial-booking flow (including template messages) is free.
- **Integration path**: rather than building an inbox, ship (1) direct Meta Cloud API for system notifications (owning the WABA), and (2) a webhook/API adapter so Respond.io/SleekFlow/Wati can read the student record and push lifecycle changes back. Twilio is not worth the USD0.005/message markup for a MY-only product.
- **AI reply drafting pattern**: "suggest reply from knowledge base, human approves" (Respond.io AI Assist) is the safe pattern for parent comms; fully autonomous AI Agents should be limited to enquiry qualification and FAQ.

## KQ2. Work management (Trello, Notion, ClickUp, Monday.com, Airtable) — kanban/pipeline/automation patterns for enrolment pipelines, teacher tasks, branch ops checklists

### Takeaway
The transferable patterns are: kanban with stage-triggered automations (Trello Butler, Monday, Kommo), card/checklist templates and calendar-recurring cards for branch ops routines (Trello), role-specific "interfaces" over one database (Airtable Interface Designer), and bundled AI that summarises and drafts inside the workspace (Notion AI in Business plan, ClickUp Brain add-on). Pricing benchmarks: Trello USD5–10/user, ClickUp USD7–12/user, Notion USD10–20/user, Airtable USD20/user, Monday USD9–32/user with 3-seat minimum — useful anchors for a per-branch or per-staff SaaS price.

### Cited Findings
- **Trello 2026:** Free (250 automation runs/mo/workspace), Standard USD5/user/mo annual (USD6 monthly; 1,000 runs/mo; unlimited Power-Ups, custom fields), Premium USD10/user/mo annual (USD12.50 monthly; unlimited runs). Butler available on all plans (as of Apr 2026); triggers = card moves, due dates, checklist completion, manual buttons; **calendar commands** create recurring cards daily/weekly/monthly/annually; **card templates** and a "Checklist Templates" card pattern for copying checklists into cards. — [usecarly Trello pricing](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/trello-pricing/); [smartprocessflow](https://smartprocessflow.com/trello-pricing); [Trello guide: automate anything](https://trello.com/guide/automate-anything); [Trello advanced features](https://trello.com/guide/enterprise/advanced-features); [TemperStack Butler guide](https://www.temperstack.com/learn/trello/set-up-butler-automation/)

### Inferences (what to steal)
- **Enrolment pipeline = kanban whose columns are the lifecycle stages**, with per-column SLA timers (e.g. "Trial booked > 3 days without confirmation → flag") and automation "buttons" on cards (Send trial reminder / Convert to enrolled / Mark lost with reason). Kommo and Trello both prove staff will use a board where the CRM is *the board*.
- **Branch ops checklists** should be *calendar-recurring card templates* (Trello pattern): daily opening/closing, weekly fee-chase, monthly LHDN consolidated e-invoice, term-start stock check — each spawning a checklist card per branch with an owner and due date, plus a HQ dashboard of completion rates across branches.
- **Teacher task tracking**: use the Airtable "one base, many interfaces" idea — one data model, with a teacher interface (my classes, attendance, progress notes), a branch-manager interface (staffing, cover requests), and an HQ interface (cross-branch KPIs). Don't build separate modules.
- **Automation quotas as a pricing lever**: every tool caps automation runs per plan (Trello 250/1,000/unlimited; Monday 250/25,000). A centre product can meter "automated WhatsApp sends" similarly, aligning price with Meta cost pass-through.
- **AI pricing anchor**: workspace AI is either bundled at the USD20/user tier (Notion) or a USD9/user add-on (ClickUp). For a centre product, bundling AI summaries into the top tier is the current market norm.

## KQ3. Booking / scheduling UX (Calendly, Acuity, Setmore, Fresha, Mindbody, Glofox, Zenoti) — class booking, waitlists, capacity, make-up credits, packages/memberships, no-show handling

### Takeaway
Gyms/salons have already solved the tuition-centre problems of capacity-limited recurring classes, waitlists that auto-fill cancellations, prepaid packages/credits with expiry and rollover rules, deposits/no-show fees, and automated reminders — all native in Glofox, Mindbody, Fresha and Zenoti. The concrete borrowables are: dynamic waitlists with configurable notify order (Fresha), auto no-show marking 10 minutes after start (Mindbody), package/credit models with rollover-or-expire rules per plan (Zenoti), deposit retained on late-cancel/no-show (Fresha), and win-back automation triggered by a no-show (Zenoti).

### Cited Findings
- **Fresha:** Intelligent Waitlist auto-fills last-minute cancellations by matching open slots to waiting clients; business chooses notify order — **first in line, high-value first, or offer-to-all (first to book wins)**. Deposits/prepayment and cancellation policies run "on autopilot"; deposit is deducted from the bill after service and **retained on late-cancel/no-show**. Automated email+SMS reminders. No permanent free plan any more. — [Fresha scheduling features](https://www.fresha.com/for-business/features/scheduling); [Fresha payment policies](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/payments/615-set-up-payment-policies); [Salon Today on Fresha deposits](https://www.salontoday.com/1080582/fresha-launches-new-deposits-feature); [Pabau Fresha review](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-review/); [Fresha pricing](https://www.fresha.com/pricing)

### Inferences (what to steal)
- **Make-up class = service credit with expiry.** Model each absence-with-notice as a "make-up credit" (Zenoti service-credit model) with a per-programme rule: rollover N months or expire at term end, visible to the parent in-app, redeemable only against classes with spare capacity. Show expiry clearly to avoid the ClassPass-style backlash.
- **Waitlist notify-order as a setting** (Fresha): "first in line" by default, "high-value first" (e.g. siblings / full-fee payers) as an option, "offer to all, first to confirm wins" for last-minute make-up slots on WhatsApp.
- **Auto no-show marking** (Mindbody's 10-minute rule) fed by teacher check-in; a no-show should trigger (a) a utility WhatsApp "we missed Ali today" and (b) a win-back/at-risk flag after N consecutive misses (Zenoti pattern).
- **Deposit / trial-fee pattern**: gyms and salons take deposits to cut no-shows; for trial classes, an RM20–50 deposit collected via FPX/DuitNow QR link and offset against the first month's fee is the direct analogue.
- **Capacity as a first-class field on every class instance**, with intro offers (trial packs) and class packs (holiday intensives) as separate product types, exactly as Glofox does natively.
- **Cancellation window + waitlist interaction** (Mindbody): stop auto-promoting from the waitlist inside the late-cancel window, otherwise promoted parents get billed for a slot they can't attend.

## KQ4. Billing / payments in Malaysia (CHIP, Billplz, ToyyibPay, senangPay, iPay88/Adaptis, Stripe MY, Curlec/Razorpay, Payex) — recurring FPX/card, DuitNow QR, e-mandate, fees

### Takeaway
For monthly tuition fees, the two real recurring rails are (1) **FPX e-mandate / direct debit** (natively offered by Curlec and reported for CHIP/Payex) and (2) **card tokenisation** (iPay88, Payex, senangPay Advance, Stripe Billing). One-off FPX is cheapest on toyyibPay (RM1 flat) and Billplz (RM1.25 Basic / RM0.75 Standard with RM999/yr), while Stripe's 3%+RM1 on FPX is the most expensive. DuitNow QR MDR is widely waived for micro/small merchants (CIMB static QR 0% extended past 30 Jun 2026), making it the cheapest walk-in counter option. All figures unverified against primary pricing pages (blocked).

## KQ5. LHDN e-Invoice (MyInvois) 2025–2026 rollout: does a tuition centre need to issue e-invoices, and what must the software do? Accounting integrations (Bukku, SQL Account, AutoCount, Xero, QuickBooks)

### Takeaway
As of **1 Sept 2026 (e-Invoice Guideline v4.8, dated 30 Aug 2026)** taxpayers with annual turnover/revenue **below RM3 million are exempt** from e-Invoice (up from RM1M in Dec 2025 and RM500k originally), unless they have a ≥RM3M non-individual shareholder/holding/related company. Most single-branch tuition centres are therefore exempt; a multi-branch group over RM3M (or with a ≥RM3M parent) must issue e-invoices, and because parents are consumers it can use **monthly consolidated e-invoices** (submitted within 7 days of month end) except for any single transaction >RM10,000, which needs an individual e-invoice from 1 Jan 2026. Software options: submit via MyInvois API directly (needs LHDN digital certificate), via middleware, or push invoices to an LHDN-certified accounting package (Bukku, SQL Account, AutoCount, Xero via Invoici, QuickBooks via integrator) that handles the API.

### Cited Findings
- LHDN published **e-Invoice Guideline Version 4.8 dated 30 August 2026**, raising the exemption threshold from **RM1M to RM3M** annual turnover/revenue, effective **1 September 2026**, following the PM's 30 Aug announcement; HASiL estimates >1.1M MSMEs exempted. Exemption applies to individuals, partnerships, companies, co-operatives, but **not** where a non-individual shareholder, holding company, related company or JV has revenue ≥RM3M. — [Comarch](https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/data-management/legal-regulation-changes/malaysia-raises-e-invoice-exemption-threshold-to-rm3-million/); [VATupdate 8 Sep 2026](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/09/08/malaysia-raises-e-invoice-exemption-threshold-to-myr-3-million/); [JomEInvoice on v4.8](https://jomeinvoice.my/article/lhdn-e-invoice-general-guideline-v4-8-rm3-million-exemption/); [Kalyx Consultants](https://www.kalyxconsultants.com.my/post/malaysia-e-invoice-2026-latest-implementation-timeline); primary PDF: [IRBM e-Invoice Guideline v4.8](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/IRBM-e-Invoice-Guideline.pdf) and [Specific Guideline v4.9](https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/IRBM-e-Invoice-Specific-Guideline.pdf) (both blocked for fetch)

### Inferences (what to steal / what the software must do)
- **Ship "e-Invoice readiness" as a tiered feature**: (a) always store buyer TIN/ID and MSIC codes so a centre can switch on e-invoicing when it crosses RM3M or is acquired by a ≥RM3M group; (b) generate a **monthly consolidated e-invoice** per branch (aggregating fee receipts) and flag any single receipt >RM10k; (c) let a parent request an individual e-invoice (e.g. for company-sponsored fees) from the receipt page.

Add a direct MyInvois API path later for groups >RM3M.
- Marketing framing: since most independent centres are now exempt, e-invoice is a *group/franchise* feature, not an SME hook.
