## Q1. Tutoring / learning-centre platforms — who they are, what they cost, which are most used and why

### Takeaway
The tutoring/learning-centre segment splits into (a) tutor-agency tools priced per lesson or per tutor (Teachworks, TutorBird, TutorCruncher, Oases) and (b) class/activity-centre tools priced flat per location or by student-count tier (Jackrabbit Class, iClassPro, Pike13, Classcard, Enrollsy, Sawyer, Omnify, Bookeo, Amilia, Regpack). Jackrabbit (12,000+ schools claimed) and iClassPro dominate the US class-based segment; TutorCruncher and Teachworks dominate the tutoring-agency segment; Kumon and Mathnasium run proprietary franchise systems (CMS / Radius) that franchisees cannot swap out, which spawns a cottage industry of third-party add-ons (Kumotic, Cheqdin, custom dashboards).

### Inferences
- Three distinct pricing archetypes are visible: per-lesson metered (Teachworks), % of revenue (TutorCruncher, Regpack), and flat per-location/per-tier (iClassPro, Enrollsy, Classcard, Pike13, Jackrabbit). Reviews consistently reward predictable flat pricing (iClassPro praised, Jackrabbit's student-count sampling resented).
- For a Malaysian tuition chain, Classcard (Dubai; WhatsApp via Twilio; Razorpay/Stripe; flat pricing; unlimited staff/students) is the closest international analogue in both geography and feature intent; TutorCruncher's $50/branch surcharge and revenue-percentage model would be expensive at Malaysian fee levels.
- Kumon and Mathnasium show that large franchise systems keep the system-of-record proprietary and thin, and franchisees bolt on CRM/retention tooling — an opening for a "franchise dashboard" layer.

### Inferences
- Common childcare parent-app UX standard (2026): (1) chronological feed of photos/videos per child, (2) structured daily report (meals/nap/toileting/health), (3) kiosk check-in via PIN + QR "quick scan" + digital signature + health screen, (4) in-app messaging with translation/AI-polish, (5) in-app invoices/autopay and subsidy visibility. A tuition centre would keep (1), (3), (4), (5) and replace (2) with a per-lesson progress note / homework log.
- Australian vendors are structurally tied to the Child Care Subsidy (CCS) system, which makes their billing engines government-specific and less portable to Malaysia; the app/UX layer is the transferable part.

## Q3. 2026 feature standard for a multi-branch operator (franchise dashboards, branch P&L, staff scheduling, teacher payroll per class)

### Takeaway
By 2026 the multi-branch baseline visible across leaders is: one login with role-based views; a cross-location executive/BI dashboard (Jackrabbit cites 30+ KPIs side-by-side; Famly live occupancy/revenue/debt by site; Kangarootime cost centres); branch-scoped users, tax and billing (TutorCruncher branches); centralised billing and payroll/time-tracking (Pike13); and per-lesson/per-student/revenue-share tutor pay calculation (TutorCruncher, Teachworks, Oases). Branch-level P&L is only explicitly claimed by childcare-oriented Kangarootime; tutoring tools stop at revenue/enrolment by branch.
